Why this website looks like this

Posted 28 October 2026

I posted about Braintree Town FC's website recently. It felt like visiting an old person's house with all of their vintage knick-knacks. I loved it. I hope they leave it alone.

I wanted this website to have that same feeling. Something very 1998, with photos, links and things to read.

You might know me from Timcast. I'm putting some of that work here, but I also want to write more about culture, travel and what's going on elsewhere in the world. There are already a few photos from Africa to have a look at. I'm working on a newsletter about Africa too.

Anyway, welcome. Have a look around.
